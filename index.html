The Journal · No. 02

About Rajveer.

The Legend Rajveer — solo motorcyclist, video diarist, member of the BUTR riding group. Based in Mumbai, riding wherever the road leads — from the lunar plateaus of Spiti to the temple coasts of Puri, from the Kashmir snowfields to the southern tip at Kanyakumari.

This site is the long-form companion to the YouTube channel. Each ride is documented in full — fuel stops, mountain weather, the dhaba where the chai tasted like petrol, the pass where the bike stalled in thin air. If even one rider plans a safer journey from these notes, it's worth it.
