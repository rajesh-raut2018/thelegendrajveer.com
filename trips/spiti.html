Mumbai to Spiti

An 18-day journey from Mumbai across the plains, up through Manali, over the Atal Tunnel and Kunzum La, into the lunar landscape of Spiti Valley. Ancient Buddhist monasteries clinging to cliffs, lone mud villages at 4,000+ metres, and roads that test every system in the bike and the rider.

The Logbook · Day-by-Day

The route, written.

01Day 01

Mumbai → Vadodara

NH48 north start 440 km Highway

Big start day. Mumbai-Pune Expressway → NH48 to Gujarat. Sleep Vadodara.

02Day 02

Vadodara → Udaipur

Aravalli foothills 350 km Lakes city

NH48 northbound. Enter Rajasthan. Evening at Lake Pichola, Udaipur.

03Day 03

Udaipur → Jaipur

Royal corridor 390 km Pink city

Through Aravalli. Sleep Jaipur. Quick Hawa Mahal stop.

04Day 04

Jaipur → Delhi → Chandigarh

NH44 north 560 km Long day

Delhi bypass. Continue to Chandigarh — gateway to Himachal.

05Day 05

Chandigarh → Manali

Himachal climb 310 km Hills begin

First serious climb. NH3 through Mandi → Kullu → Manali. Cool air after 4 days of heat.

06Day 06

Manali — rest + acclimatise

Mall Road · Hadimba 0 km Rest

Mandatory acclimatisation. Local Mall Road. Hadimba Temple. Bike service check.

07Day 07

Manali → Kaza (Spiti)

★ Atal Tunnel + Kunzum La 200 km EPIC day

The day. Atal Tunnel cuts through. Then over Kunzum La (4,551m). First view of lunar Spiti Valley. Sleep Kaza.

08Day 08

Kaza rest + Key Monastery

Acclimatise at 3,800m 30 km Rest

Body adjusts to 3,800m. Key Monastery — one of the most photogenic in India. Local exploration only.

09Day 09

Kaza → Komic → Hikkim → Kaza

World's highest village 70 km Day ride

Komic — world's highest motorable village (4,587m). Hikkim — world's highest post office. Send a postcard home from the top of the world.

10Day 10

Kaza → Tabo → Dhankar

Ancient monasteries 75 km Slow day

Tabo Monastery (1,000+ yrs old). Dhankar — clifftop fortress monastery. Spiti at its most surreal.

11Day 11

Kaza → Kalpa

Kinnaur descent 210 km Mountain road

Spiti to Kinnaur via Nako. Kinnaur Kailash range at sunset from Kalpa. Apple country.

12Day 12

Kalpa → Shimla

Hindustan-Tibet road 240 km Heritage road

Old Hindustan-Tibet road. Through Recong Peo, Sangla. Sleep Shimla.

13Day 13

Shimla → Chandigarh → Delhi

Plains return 390 km Heat re-entry

Down the hills. Through Chandigarh to Delhi outskirts. Heat hits hard after 8 days in mountains.

14Day 14

Delhi → Jaipur → Ajmer

Rajasthan again 450 km Highway

NH48 south. Jaipur lunch. Sleep Ajmer.

15Day 15

Ajmer → Ahmedabad

Plains push 490 km Hot day

Long Rajasthan-Gujarat day. Sleep Ahmedabad.

16Day 16

Ahmedabad → Surat → Mumbai

HOME! 440 km Final leg

NH48 expressway. Mumbai by evening. 18 days · 5,200 km · Spiti and back complete ✓
