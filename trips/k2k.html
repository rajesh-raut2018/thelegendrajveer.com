Kashmir to Kanyakumari

The Indian motorcyclist's ultimate pilgrimage. From the cool deodar forests of Kashmir, down the spine of the country, through Punjab plains, the Deccan plateau, the lush Western Ghats, and finally to the rock where three seas meet. ~3,800 km of country crossed in 12 days.

The Logbook · Day-by-Day

The route, written.

01Day 01

Srinagar arrival + bike pickup

Dal Lake · Srinagar 0 km Recovery

Land in Srinagar. Pick up bike. Float on Dal Lake shikara. Acclimatise — first night in the Himalayas before the long descent south.

02Day 02

Srinagar → Jammu

via Banihal · NH44 270 km Mountain descent

First ride of the trip. Jawahar Tunnel descent through Banihal. Cool mountains to plains. Big elevation drop. Camp at Jammu.

03Day 03

Jammu → Amritsar

Wagah Border evening 220 km Punjab plains

NH44 through Punjab. Evening at Wagah Border for the flag ceremony. Sleep in Amritsar, dinner from Golden Temple langar.

04Day 04

Amritsar → Delhi

Golden Quadrilateral 450 km Highway day

Long expressway day. Punjab → Haryana → Delhi NCR. Stay near outskirts, avoid Delhi city traffic.

05Day 05

Delhi → Jaipur

Pink City stop 280 km Rajasthan begins

NH48. Enter Rajasthan. Evening at Hawa Mahal, Jaipur old city. Sleep near City Palace.

06Day 06

Jaipur → Udaipur

Aravalli range 390 km Lakes & forts

Through the Aravalli range. Arrive Udaipur — the City of Lakes. Sunset at Lake Pichola.

07Day 07

Udaipur → Mumbai

Coastal approach 750 km Long highway

Long descent into Maharashtra. NH48 expressway. Cross Vapi → Surat. Crash in Mumbai. Quick service stop.

08Day 08

Mumbai → Goa

Konkan coast NH66 590 km Coastal cruise

Down the Konkan coast on NH66. Mahad → Ratnagiri → Sawantwadi → Goa border. Sleep in North Goa.

09Day 09

Goa → Mangalore

Karnataka coast 370 km Karnataka begins

Continue NH66. Murudeshwar Shiva statue stop. Udupi dosa lunch. Sleep Mangalore.

10Day 10

Mangalore → Kozhikode

Kerala coast 230 km Easy day

Cross into Kerala. Kannur drive-on beach. Malabar biryani dinner in Calicut.

11Day 11

Kozhikode → Trivandrum

Long Kerala day 440 km South push

Long Kerala day via Alleppey backwaters glimpse, Varkala cliff beach stop. Sleep in Trivandrum.

12Day 12

Trivandrum → KANYAKUMARI

★ TIP OF INDIA 90 km Mission complete

Last short leg. Cross into Tamil Nadu. Arrive Kanyakumari. Vivekananda Rock. Where three seas meet. Sunset over the Arabian Sea. K2K complete ✓
