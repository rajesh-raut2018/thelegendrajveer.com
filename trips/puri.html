Mumbai → Puri → Mumbai

A focused 10-day round-trip from Mumbai to Jagannath Puri on the Odisha coast. Through Maharashtra plains, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh coast, into Odisha, the magnificent Jagannath Temple, the Konark Sun Temple, then the long ride home through Chhattisgarh.

The Logbook · Day-by-Day

The route, written.

01Day 01

Mumbai → Hyderabad

Long highway start 710 km Big start

Mumbai-Pune Expressway then NH65 east. Long first day. Sleep Hyderabad outskirts.

02Day 02

Hyderabad → Vijayawada

Telangana → AP 270 km Easy day

Cross into AP. Sleep Vijayawada beside the Krishna river.

03Day 03

Vijayawada → Visakhapatnam

AP coast 350 km Coast hits

First sight of the Bay of Bengal. NH16 to Vizag. Sleep at RK Beach.

04Day 04

Visakhapatnam → Bhubaneswar

Enter Odisha 440 km Long coastal

Cross into Odisha. Sleep Bhubaneswar. Lingaraj Temple darshan if evening.

05Day 05

Bhubaneswar → Puri

★ JAGANNATH PURI 65 km Mission day

Short ride to Jagannath Puri. Darshan at Lord Jagannath Temple. Beach evening. Mission complete ✓

06Day 06

Puri → Konark → Puri

Sun Temple day 70 km Heritage

Day trip to Konark Sun Temple. Marvel at the stone chariot. Back to Puri for sunset.

07Day 07

Puri → Raipur

Return begins 560 km Long day

Long inland push through southern Odisha into Chhattisgarh. Sleep Raipur.

08Day 08

Raipur → Nagpur

Through MP 300 km Easy day

Cross into Maharashtra via Vidarbha. Sleep Nagpur.

09Day 09

Nagpur → Aurangabad

Maharashtra interior 450 km Highway

Through Vidarbha to Marathwada. Sleep Aurangabad. Some choose Ajanta-Ellora detour.

10Day 10

Aurangabad → Mumbai

HOME! 335 km Final leg

NH160 → Nashik → Mumbai. 10 days · 3,400 km · Jagannath darshan complete ✓
