Nepal Off-Road

My first ever international ride — taking the bike across the border into Nepal with the BUTR riding group. Extreme off-road sections, river crossings, mountain passes deep in the Himalayan foothills, and the kind of brotherhood that only forms when ten riders share the same dust at 3,500 metres.

The Logbook · Day-by-Day

The route, written.

01Day 01

Mumbai → Indore

BUTR ride begins 590 km Highway start

All 10 BUTR riders meet at Mumbai. Convoy out via Nashik. Sleep Indore. Brotherhood formed at the first dhaba dinner.

02Day 02

Indore → Sagar

Central plains 390 km Highway

NH27 east. MP plains. Sleep Sagar.

03Day 03

Sagar → Lucknow

UP heartland 440 km Highway

Through Bundelkhand into UP. Sleep Lucknow. Tunday kebab dinner.

04Day 04

Lucknow → Gorakhpur

Approach the border 260 km Easy day

Eastern UP. Sleep Gorakhpur — last Indian town before Nepal.

05Day 05

Gorakhpur → Pokhara (Nepal!)

★ FIRST BORDER CROSSING 270 km International

Cross Sunauli border. Carnet papers + insurance. First time taking the bike to a foreign country. Reach Pokhara by evening. Sleep beside Phewa Lake.

06Day 06

Pokhara rest + paragliding

Annapurna views 0 km Rest

Sarangkot for sunrise Annapurna views. Paragliding off Sarangkot. Phewa Lake boating.

07Day 07

Pokhara → Beni → Tatopani

Off-road begins 120 km Off-road

Leave the highway. Dirt road towards Mustang. Hot springs at Tatopani. The off-road challenge begins.

08Day 08

Tatopani → Kagbeni

★ Mustang region 85 km Extreme off-road

Hardest day. River crossings. Loose gravel. Steep climbs. Kali Gandaki gorge. Enter Upper Mustang region — semi-arid Tibetan landscape.

09Day 09

Kagbeni → Muktinath

★ Sacred temple 30 km Steep climb

Climb to Muktinath Temple (3,710m). Both Hindu and Buddhist pilgrimage site. 108 water spouts. Sacred fire.

10Day 10

Muktinath → Pokhara

Return descent 150 km Hard return

Same off-road in reverse. Tougher in some sections — going downhill on loose rock. Back to Pokhara.

11Day 11

Pokhara → Kathmandu

Capital arrival 200 km Mountain road

Prithvi Highway. Winding mountain road. Arrive Kathmandu by evening. Thamel district.

12Day 12

Kathmandu rest

Durbar Square + Patan 0 km Culture day

Pashupatinath temple. Boudhanath stupa. Durbar Square. Patan. Most photogenic city of the trip.

13Day 13

Kathmandu → Birgunj → Patna

Border re-cross 440 km Long day

Cross Birgunj border back to India. Sleep Patna. Litti chokha dinner.

14Day 14

Patna → Varanasi

Ganga arrival 260 km Spiritual

Easy day. Evening Ganga aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat. Most powerful sacred experience of the trip.

15Day 15

Varanasi → Jabalpur

MP push 560 km Big day

Long ride south. Sleep Jabalpur.

16Day 16

Jabalpur → Mumbai

HOME! 950 km (split) Final 2 days

Final push (some riders split this into 2 days). 16 days · 4,400 km · First international BUTR ride complete ✓
